# Signal2Ship Concept

## One-Line Definition

Signal2Ship turns fresh market signals into narrow, shippable app MVPs.

## Product Thesis

Recent public conversations reveal what people are struggling with before traditional reports do. `/last30days` is useful because it searches across communities, social platforms, GitHub, prediction markets, and the web, then synthesizes what people actually discuss.

Signal2Ship adds the missing productization layer:

1. Preserve evidence in a structured ledger.
2. Separate hype from repeated pain.
3. Score opportunities against buildability and founder fit.
4. Force MVP scope reduction.
5. Stop for explicit final idea/MVP spec approval.
6. Build and verify a preview app only from the approved packet.
7. Prepare deployment-readiness artifacts.
8. Stop before production launch unless the user approves.

## Non-Goals For v1

- No marketplace.
- No team collaboration.
- No payments.
- No autonomous production launch.
- No generic agent platform.
- No broad dashboard unless the selected MVP specifically needs one.

## Success Criteria For v1

- A user can provide a domain and goal.
- The workflow produces an evidence ledger with source-backed signals.
- The workflow ranks opportunities and selects one.
- The selected opportunity becomes a narrow MVP spec approval packet.
- The workflow stops before app creation until that packet is explicitly approved.
- A generated app can be built and tested from the approved packet.
- Deployment-readiness artifacts are prepared, but no deploy, store submission,
  payment activation, real-user-data collection, or public launch posting runs
  without explicit approval.
